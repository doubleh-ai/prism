# Prism v0.15.0

## Added

- Vibe Trading workbench for turning a loose market intuition into a structured research plan.
- `/api/vibe/plan`, `/api/vibe/plans`, and `/api/vibe/plans/{plan_id}` for account-scoped saved plans.
- Vibe candidate actions: add to watchlist, open market view, run one-click analysis, or hand the plan to chat for deeper research.

## Changed

- Bumped app static assets to `v=45`.
- Service Worker cache moved to `prism-v0150` and now pre-caches `app.vibe.js`.

## Fixed

- A-share Vibe plans now only treat six-digit stock codes as A-share tickers, so theme acronyms such as `AI`, `CPO`, or `HBM` are not inserted as fake candidates.

## Security

- Vibe Trading is research-only. It does not place trades, connect brokerage accounts, or provide investment advice.
